01The challenge

Present a broad connected system in a way that lets a small transport business quickly understand what it is, who it is for and how to test it.

02The response

A guided product story built around the real Office + Driver workflow, supported by real interface imagery, direct pricing and practical installation routes.

03The result

A complete public product website with clear product, tour, Office, Driver, audience, pricing, download, installation and support journeys.

Product positioning

Lead with the working day, not a feature inventory.

The central message focuses on running the fleet and keeping people moving. That gives visitors an immediate operational frame before introducing the software architecture.

The supporting structure separates the two sides of the workflow: FleetSlate Office for the wider operation and FleetSlate Driver for focused work at the vehicle. The website then connects planning, tracking, checks, evidence and records back into that same story.

Core positioning
One clear system connecting office planning, drivers, vehicles and the evidence behind daily fleet work.

Information architecture

Give every decision its own clear route.

Product overview

A concise explanation of the connected system and its main operational value.

Real product tour

Interface-led content showing the system rather than relying on abstract claims.

Office and Driver pages

Dedicated pages for different users while preserving one joined-up product story.

Audience and pricing

Clear fit, 30-day trial and one-time Lifetime pricing without package confusion.

Installation and downloads

Practical routes for Windows, macOS, Android, iPhone and iPad.

Support and delivery

Purchase delivery, installation guidance, contact and customer support kept visible.

Design decisions

A confident product identity without visual noise.

High-contrast hierarchy

Dark surfaces, bright green accents and strong typography make important choices easy to scan.

Interface-led credibility

Real product screens support the claims and reduce the distance between marketing and the actual software.

Responsive sales journey

The same product story remains clear across desktop and mobile, with trial and product routes always close at hand.

Search foundation

Built around real fleet-management questions.

The website uses descriptive page titles, canonical URLs, structured headings, internal links, meaningful image text, FAQ content, robots rules and an XML sitemap. Product pages are organised around terms and questions a potential customer would genuinely use.

The SEO work supports discovery without turning the copy into a list of repeated keywords.
