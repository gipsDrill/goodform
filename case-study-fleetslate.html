OFFICE + DRIVER · ONE WORKFLOW

Project at a glance

Product website, customer journey and visual system.

The work brought FleetSlate's real product, trial, downloads, installation and purchase journey into one coherent public website.

Sector
Fleet management software
Audience
Small transport and fleet operations
Platforms
Windows, macOS, iOS and Android
Focus
Clarity, trust and conversion

The challenge

Show the complete system without creating more clutter.

Fleet software can quickly become a wall of features. The site instead shows how Office and Driver work together, where the practical value sits, and what a new customer should do first.

01

Make the workflow visible

Planning, tracking, records, checks and evidence are presented as one connected daily operation.

02

Use the real product

Product-tour sections and interface imagery keep the promise tied to what customers actually receive.

03

Reduce launch friction

Trial, installation, supported devices and pricing are placed in a logical path rather than scattered across the site.

What was built

A full product story from first visit to first working day.

Product positioning

A direct message centred on connected Office + Driver operations, not generic software claims.

Responsive product pages

Dedicated routes for the product, tour, Office, Driver, use cases, pricing and support.

Trial and download journey

Clear calls to action, download routes, installation guidance and supported-device information.

SEO foundation

Structured metadata, useful page content, internal linking, sitemap and canonical handling.

Trust and delivery

Pricing, seller details, support, privacy and delivery information are accessible before purchase.

Mobile usability

Navigation, product cards and calls to action remain clear on phones and smaller screens.

Design direction

Technical enough to feel capable. Calm enough to feel approachable.

The visual system uses dark product imagery, clean light sections, restrained green accents and strong spacing. Information is grouped into short decisions rather than long specification lists.

Real interface firstShort, useful sectionsOne primary actionConsistent product languageDesktop and mobile parity

Result

A coherent public home for a working software product.

FleetSlate now has a website that introduces the product, demonstrates the Office + Driver workflow, answers practical purchase questions and guides visitors into the full trial without overstating what the system does.
