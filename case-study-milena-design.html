Case study · Milena Design

A specialist manufacturer presented with clarity and confidence.

Milena Design needed a professional website for business customers seeking garment manufacturing, textile production, sampling and contract sewing in the UK.

01The challenge

Explain a broad manufacturing capability to brands, wholesalers and distributors while making it clear that the company serves business customers rather than retail buyers.

02The response

A structured one-page experience covering company credibility, product categories, manufacturing services, sampling, business-only positioning, gallery and enquiries.

03The result

A responsive manufacturing website with a clear B2B message, direct contact route and search content aligned with real garment and textile services.

Content strategy

Make the manufacturing offer easy to understand.

The content begins with the strongest category: garment and knitted textile manufacturing. It then establishes the company’s history, Greater Manchester production base and business-customer focus.

Products and services are separated so buyers can understand both what Milena Design can make and how the working relationship can be structured — bespoke manufacturing, private label, contract sewing, Cut Make and Trim, samples and ongoing production.

Audience clarity
Built for clothing brands, manufacturers, wholesalers, retailers and distributors — not individual retail orders.

Page structure

A compact site with enough depth for serious enquiries.

Credibility first

Established in 2015, family-run and based in Greater Manchester.

Product categories

Children’s clothing, nursery goods, knitted products and soft furnishings.

Manufacturing services

Bespoke, private label, contract sewing, CMT, production and sampling.

Business-only message

A visible qualification that reduces irrelevant retail enquiries.

Visual proof

Product and workshop imagery supports the written manufacturing claims.

Direct enquiry route

Address, email, phone and an uncomplicated manufacturing enquiry form.

Design decisions

Professional enough for buyers, warm enough for a family business.

Product-led opening

A strong garment image and concise manufacturing headline establish the category immediately.

Clear B2B hierarchy

Services, products and audience information are organised for procurement and brand enquiries.

Responsive simplicity

The full offer remains readable and easy to contact from phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Local and service SEO

Specific enough for the right manufacturing searches.

The site uses clear headings and detailed FAQ answers around private label clothing, baby and children’s clothing, sampling, CMT, bespoke textiles and UK manufacturing.

Location information connects the company with Ashton-under-Lyne, Tameside and Greater Manchester while the copy makes clear that business customers are served across the UK.

  • Garment and textile manufacturing
  • Private label clothing production
  • Cut, Make and Trim services
  • Sampling and prototype development
  • Ashton-under-Lyne and Tameside
  • UK business manufacturing enquiries
