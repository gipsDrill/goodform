Case study · Milena Design

A manufacturing business presented with clarity and confidence.

B2B MANUFACTURING · UK

Project at a glance

A focused website for a specialist UK manufacturer.

The site turns a broad production capability into clear product groups, manufacturing services and a business-only enquiry route.

Sector
Garment and textile manufacturing
Audience
Brands, wholesalers and business customers
Location
Ashton-under-Lyne, Greater Manchester
Focus
Products, services and enquiries

The challenge

Explain specialist manufacturing to buyers who need detail quickly.

Visitors needed to understand what Milena Design makes, which services are available, whether the company works with business customers like them, and how to start a useful production conversation.

01

Separate products clearly

Children's clothing, nursery goods, knitted products and soft furnishings are easy to scan.

02

Clarify manufacturing services

Bespoke manufacturing, private label, CMT, contract sewing and samples are described in plain business language.

03

Qualify the enquiry

Business-only positioning helps avoid retail enquiries and directs the right customers to the contact form.

What was built

A complete route from capability to contact.

Manufacturing-led homepage

Experience, location and B2B positioning are established before the product detail begins.

Product architecture

Core garment and textile categories are grouped so buyers can recognise relevant capability quickly.

Service descriptions

Production, private label, sewing, CMT and sample-making services are explained without jargon.

Production gallery

Visual evidence supports the written message and helps the site feel grounded in a real facility.

Search content

Useful manufacturing FAQs support specific searches without turning the page into repetitive keyword copy.

Responsive contact route

Business details and an enquiry form remain easy to use across desktop and mobile screens.

Design direction

Professional, warm and product-led.

The design balances a clean manufacturing presentation with soft textile imagery and restrained colour. Strong headings and generous spacing keep a detailed offer readable.

B2B message firstReal product categoriesVisible local credibilityClear enquiry routeMobile-first reading

Result

A clearer digital front door for business customers.

The live website gives buyers a structured overview of Milena Design's manufacturing capability, answers common production questions and makes it straightforward to start an informed enquiry.
